## Building for Kohl Center

### What plays where

- Kohl Center hosts men's and women's basketball, men's hockey and some volleyball. Women's hockey is at LaBahn.
- Basketball uses every board. Men's hockey drops the scorer's table, baseline table and stanchion; leave those off hockey projects.
- Special events (Steak Fry and similar) usually run the main, fascia, 300s, tunnels and top ring only. Ask which boards the event's production plan schedules before building the tables or ring sponsor/corner windows.
- Kohl Center boards play in sync with each other: no propagation delays between boards.

### Centerhung main

- One main feed shows on all four sides of the centerhung.
- The template's Kohl Center main comp has an old height. Resize it to `kohl-center/centerhung-main` (1104×624, ⚠ conflict) before you build, and refit anything nested in it.
- HD versions of the main are requested often. Build them as their own comp (duplicate the card, resize, scale positions and sizes to the new width). Don't nest the native main at a scale, and don't use the North Board main as the HD base (its safe area is offset). The `kohl-center/main-16-9-feed` (1920×1080, ⚠ unconfirmed, conflict) board itself is still unconfirmed.
- Turning an existing HD loop into a main file: fill-crop it to the main size and keep the source frame rate and audio.
- The PlayerID intro slot uses footage at native pixels, so stills must be wide HD frames with the subject in the middle band.
- Headshot intros: one strong zoom-parallax on the transition, then hold dead still. No dip from black.
- Roster slides: eyes on the upper third line. Staff slides are a header and a plain list, one column.
- Overlays for the main render as QuickTime with alpha; opaque mains as H.264 (mains often at 40 Mbps).
- In Display Studio, zone names under Main drop their "Main-" prefix (same for "Ring-" and "Fascia-").

### Top ring and the main as one piece

- The main and the top ring can share one background: stack the ring above the main and let each side's gradient flow up from the main into its ring face.
- The ring is four faces and four corners in one loop, with one face sitting on the seam. Face and corner positions are in the top ring's manifest notes; the seam face needs a wrap twin at the far end.
- Design the ring as one full render of four seamless faces. A tile must be x-independent or carry wrap twins.
- Scroll slowly. Colosseum's spec says the ring scrolls over 15 s; in practice every ring scroll has been called too fast and halved, ending around one face pitch per 30 s (2592 px a minute). Default slow.
- Full systems: no type on the ring unless asked; in highlight sections the ring can carry a sharp slice of the main, one copy per face pair, meeting on the corners.
- Event loops may put a wordmark or text on ring faces (Steak Fry, Garding Against Cancer). Logos on the ring stay static. Faces may stack type; don't put two logos on one face.
- Ring sponsor windows (`kohl-center/centerhung-top-ring/sponsor-north-fg` (1056×72, ⚠ conflict) ×4) and corners are separate deliverables; leave them plain red if the request doesn't include them.

### 360 fascia

- Deliver four zone files only: `kohl-center/360-fascia/sideline-extended-east-fg` (11472×72, ⚠ conflict), `kohl-center/360-fascia/sideline-extended-west-fg` (11472×72, ⚠ conflict) and `kohl-center/360-fascia/endzone-gip-fg` (1104×72, ⚠ conflict) for north and south. Together they cover the whole ring. Don't render the full fascia canvas unless the board operator asks; the zones are what plays. If a full canvas is ever needed, assemble it from the four files.
- Older projects (WBB loops) split the fascia into sideline and endzone pieces instead. That split is retired; don't copy it.
- Build one continuous ring master at the full ring length, then cut the zones from it. Per-zone gradients look broken at the corners.
- Every zone file stands alone: no text or logo crosses a cut, and each zone's layout is centred and balanced inside it. Only the background gradient runs continuously across cuts.
- Anchor sideline content on center court (position in the fascia's manifest notes: the ring model). The end stat windows belong to the Sideline Extended files; keep text clear of them.
- The endzone is one lockup centred on the GIP, never two.
- One line of type on the fascia; never stack. Caps around 40 px on the fascia height read well.
- Never let a stretch go fully black; it reads as the LED being off. Keep a floor (about 45% in light rigs).
- A mirrored copy of one Sideline Extended file for both sides is fine when the design is symmetric.
- Full systems: type lives on the sideline fascia only, small and around 75% opacity, left-justified at its final width so reveals don't shift. East follows the main's left box-light wall, West the right wall (built mirrored). The compass naming of the ring (E-N-W-S order) is assumed, not confirmed.
- Motion Tile can't produce the full sideline width (output width × layer width must stay under 30,000 px) and fails silently. Build light bars as repeated copies instead.
- A fascia "mod map" is a PNG, never a movie.

### 300 level and tunnels

- 300 corners (`kohl-center/300-level-tunnels/corner-fullscreen-se-fg` (800×64, ⚠ conflict)) are a one-line strip: W, message, W. Corner features (`kohl-center/300-level-tunnels/corner-left-se-fg` (165×64, ⚠ conflict)) are odd-width: H.264 drops a column and corrupts the last row, so render lossless and crop one column, or deliver QuickTime Animation at full width. Colosseum expects corner features with alpha; confirm which the operator wants.
- Tunnels (`kohl-center/300-level-tunnels/tunnel-se-fg` (288×72, ⚠ conflict)) may stack two short lines; logos read small here, so give them a bigger slot.
- Full systems: no type on the 300s or tunnels unless asked.

### Tables and stanchion (basketball)

- The scorer's table `kohl-center/scorers-table` (2016×126, ⚠ conflict), baseline table `kohl-center/baseline-table` (840×126, ⚠ conflict) and stanchion `kohl-center/stanchion` (240×120, ⚠ conflict) are basketball only.
- Tables cycle one message at a time with the ambient per-letter move; the scorer's table carries a W at each end. Colosseum prefers logos only on the baseline and logo only on the stanchion (text is hard to read there).

### Loops

- Ambient and practice loops run 2:00 (they're on for hours). Event welcome loops run about 60 s; shorten to 30 s when asked. Every loop opens and closes empty.

### Look

- Linear gradients over radial "orbs" on these flat boards.
- Grain: Noise HLS (grain type, lightness about 0.05), static, so loop seams match.
- The project blends in linear sRGB: low opacities and effect amounts read much stronger in dark tones, so judge from a render frame, not from slider values.
- Sponsors: look in the Learfield Box first (Signage > Logos for Main Videoboard), then the share's sponsorship logos. On red, use all-white co-brand logos.

### Rendering template projects

- aerender crashes on the template's `•<Family> - All` sheet. Reduce the project to the comps you need on a temporary copy, then render.

## Rules for every venue

### Starting a project

- Start every multi-board project from the Wisconsin Board Template (`Wisconsin Board Template.aet`): copy it to `<Project>.aep`, then run `instantiate-family.jsx` with the family name. It renames comps, the folder, labels and expression bodies together. Never rename by hand; a renamed comp does not rewrite the expressions that point at it, and a broken expression silently falls back to its static value.
- Add boards the template lacks with the Board Builder. It reads the pinned manifest, never saves, and is safe to rerun.
- Every board comp starts as a `BOARD BASE` solid in athletics red sized to the comp. Build the look once in `01 - Inputs` and nest it, so one edit reaches every board.
- Name comps `<Family> - <Board> - <Variant>`; prefix output comps with `•`. Inputs are `<Family> - Logo - <Which>` and `Util - <What>`. Spell it LaBahn, not Labahn.
- Control or settings comps referenced only by expression report `usedIn 0`. Never delete them as unused.
- Put operator instructions in guide-layer text inside the comp, not in a README or script.

### Motion

- Hits land exactly on the audio. Every board goes black on the same frame.
- Aux boards follow the main: same brightness, colour and strobes, frame for frame. No travelling light sweeps or wipes across the aux boards.
- No scale breathing on type, logos or groups, on any board. Remove any you find. (The 2026 Steak Fry main is the one kept exception; ask before adding it anywhere.)
- Constantly looping boards use the quiet ambient move: a per-letter opacity cascade with a small drop (about 10 px), ease-out in and ease-in out. No tracking, motion blur or sheen. Each loop opens and closes empty.
- Carry impact with motion and blur, not with rings, sweeps or added graphic devices. When asked for "no effects", strip it back to clean type and timing.
- Keep flashes at or under 3 per second. "Make the peak harder" means more motion, not more flashes.
- Use the plate's own light; don't re-grade or dim real plates.
- Signature write-ons are linear, no easing, 1.0 to 2.0 s.

### Type

- Aux type is quiet: small, around 75% opacity, and on the fascias only. No type on top rings, 300-level corners or tunnels unless asked.
- Headlines: Wisconsin or Aeternus Tall (Heavy/ExtraBold, Height axis 800 or more; PostScript names `AeternusHeavy-Height…`). Sub-heads Gotham Ultra. Gotham weights in use: Ultra, Black, Bold, Medium, Book; no Light, no italics. Dharma is retired.
- Preflight fonts by PostScript name: the share's `GothamHTF-Ultra` is not `Gotham-Ultra`.
- PlayerID uses the original jersey font, never the normalised Jersey Font Cleanup builds (they shrink and lift last names).

### Colour and logos

- Athletics red is #A00000, never campus #C5050C. Red and white lead; black, dark red #900000 and greys stay under about 20% of a graphic.
- Use logos exactly as supplied. The only allowed change is red fill to white on the plain Block W. Block W + Bucky is never modified. No keylines, strokes, outlines or new colours.
- Full-colour Motion W (with stroke) is the default; the white no-stroke W is for solid red only. Full-colour logo on white, white logo on red. Don't stack two logos.

### Sizes and frame rates

- Every size comes from the manifest (this file's Boards section). Never from last season's render, an old project or a vendor file.
- Build each board at its native size. If someone asks for an HD master instead, that's their call; note it and keep the native comp as well.
- Frame rate: use the board's `fps`. When it is null or in conflict, ask; the house default for AE work is 59.94.
- Vendor (196_WISC) comps often arrive at 30 or 29.97. Conform on purpose; don't let them set the project rate.
- Known exception: the 2026 PlayerID master runs at 29.97 on every board by choice. Don't "fix" it.

### Render and delivery

- No proof renders unless asked. Verify by read-back, save, and tell the person what to scrub to.
- Before any aerender run, find comps using the Cinema 4D renderer and fix them (delete junk comps, or switch to Classic 3D / Advanced 3D).
- Render the work area, not the comp length. Purge all caches before judging frames on ribbon and fascia comps; they serve stale frames.
- Opaque boards: H.264 .mp4 at 20 Mbps, no audio unless the board takes audio. Overlays: QuickTime with alpha. H.264 needs even widths.
- Full-size stills: JPG at native size, quality 95, under about 10 MB. PNG only for flat or alpha graphics.
- Name renders after their comp. Version masters `_v01`, `_v02`. Superseded files move to `_superseded/` with a date, never overwritten in place.
- The share holds only the .aep in `_PROJECT/`, linked media in `_ASSETS/` and renders in `_EXPORTS/`. Never scripts or READMEs.
- Render locally first; copy to the share's `_EXPORTS` once approved, and verify the copy byte for byte.
- Every Badger 5 (What Year Was It) render is also copied to Box `Footage Requests/Badger 5/`.
