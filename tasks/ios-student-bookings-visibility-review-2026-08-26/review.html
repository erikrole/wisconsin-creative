What changed, and why

The list opened too narrowly

Was

Students landed on their own active bookings, so the Bookings tab did not match the shared operational view used by staff and admins.

Now

Students land on All visible active bookings and can still switch back to Mine when they need a personal view.

Read visibility was mixed across endpoints

Was

Student requester scoping was applied to the combined list, checkout list, reservation list, change signal, and booking detail read.

Now

Internal Student reads are team-visible across those paths; collaborator privacy, own-only Student mutations, kiosk custody, and personal iOS Home scope remain separate.
