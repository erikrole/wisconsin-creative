What changed, and why

The secondary line now carries operational context

Was

Item names were previewed under the booking title. With several items, the line became a truncated list and still omitted the person responsible for the checkout.

Now

The line reads `Requester · n item(s)`, with correct singular/plural grammar. The accessibility label also names the holder and count, while the detail drawer remains the complete item-manifest surface.

Remaining dashboard decisions

  • The due pills still use absolute weekday/date text even when a checkout is due today. A follow-up could use `Due today · 12:00 PM` or a short relative countdown for near deadlines.
  • The stat tiles are real filters, but the filter glyph is subtle. A follow-up could label the control group or make the active-list relationship explicit without adding another dashboard section.
  • No change was made to those two follow-ups in this slice; they need a deliberate copy/interaction decision rather than being bundled into the row subtitle fix.
