What changed, and why

Short dates carry the useful calendar context

Was

Today and tomorrow were written as `Due today` / `Due tomorrow`, while later dates used only a weekday in the compact dashboard pill.

Now

The dashboard says `Today` or `Tomorrow`, and all other dates retain a short `Mon, Aug 15`-style date before the time. Near-deadline and overdue wording is unchanged.

Selection no longer borrows urgency red

Was

Selected stat tiles used brand red for the number, icon, border, and underline, competing with Return actions and overdue state.

Now

Selection is a neutral lifted surface with a white outline, underline, and icon. Overdue counts remain red when their meaning is actually urgent.

Verification

  • 19 focused Vitest source-contract tests pass across the idle dashboard, sleep-mode removal, and iOS quality contracts.
  • WisconsinKiosk Xcode build and run succeeds on the iPad Air 11-inch (M4) simulator, iOS 26.5.
  • Runtime snapshots confirm `Today · 12:00 PM`, `Mon, Aug 31 · 12:00 PM`, and neutral selection in both Checkouts and Items Out states.

Remaining proof boundaries

  • The fixture currently shows the today and future-date branches. The short `Due in …` and `Overdue · …` branches remain source-tested and build-verified, but still deserve a live fixture/device replay during kiosk QA.
  • No API, schema, custody, or detail-surface behavior changed.
