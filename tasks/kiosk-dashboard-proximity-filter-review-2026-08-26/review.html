What changed, and why

Timing now follows operational proximity

Was

Absolute date text made a checkout due later today look like an ordinary future date and did not tell staff how close an overdue item was to its deadline.

Now

Dashboard-only copy uses `Due now`, `Due in …`, `Due today`, `Due tomorrow`, short weekday/date forms, and `Overdue · …`. Full timestamps remain on detail and edit surfaces.

The metric row now explains its interaction

Was

The tiles looked like read-only KPI cards, with the filter affordance carried mostly by a small icon.

Now

A visible instruction sits above the tiles and the filter icon has stronger weight; the existing button states and accessibility hints remain intact.
