Complete Was / Now ledger

The entries below cover all 75 findings from tasks/audit-hig-ios.md. Each group states the reader-facing cost that existed and the source-level behavior now present. Where the product deliberately remains unchanged, the reason and decision are named instead of being presented as a silent pass.

Navigation, modality, and keyboard · P1-210, P1-219–220, P2-282, P2-286–288, P2-292, P2-306, P2-308, P2-330–332

Was

Back competed with cancellation, modal tasks stacked or ended in lone Done buttons, recovery labels and titles varied, validation could strand keyboard focus on a disabled field, role preview looked like disclosure, scene recreation returned to Home, and Settings or the Mac companion had no standard keyboard/visibility path.

Now

Native navigation owns Back while Cancel/Close own dismissal; confirmable work is grouped by task; recovery and action-sheet language is compact; registration skips disabled fields and validates inline; the role-preview control is an immediate action; scene and destination state restore; Command-Comma reaches Settings; and the Mac menu-bar entry is user-controlled.

Controls, layout, and menus · P1-217–218, P1-221–224, P1-236, P2-283–285, P2-294, P2-299–305, P2-309, P2-313–314, P2-329, P2-333–334

Was

Frequent actions and calendar days fell below the 44-point target, kiosk identity rows did not scale with accessibility sizes, quarter-hour menus were unwieldy, a disabled field interrupted keyboard progression, lifecycle and copy actions were hidden behind long press, selection state was hand-drawn, a reversible license return looked destructive, color alone carried venue meaning, sliders exposed opaque numbers, and labels/haptics varied across equivalent controls.

Now

Interactive surfaces use the 44-point floor and an accessibility-size single-column kiosk roster; time selection is bounded; row lifecycle and copy actions have visible alternatives; Toggle and Picker own native selection state; calendar labels expose venue counts; crop sliders speak directionally; menus use title style and ellipses; conditional commands hide when unavailable; action-sheet titles stay compact; avatars scale from an 11-point floor; checkout says Reserve; recovery says Retry; and redundant app-authored haptics are removed.

Account erasure, privacy, and inclusive writing · P1-225, P2-289–290, P2-310, P2-315

Was

Delete Account only deactivated access, the sign-in barrier explained mechanics rather than benefit, passkey copy assumed Face ID or Touch ID, some prompts used implementation language or a collective we, and camera permission copy lacked a pre-alert convention.

Now

Self-delete pseudonymizes retained custody/audit attribution, removes direct identity and authentication data, deletes notification/device/preferences and passkeys, revokes companion access, and records the retention boundary; sign-in explains the person-facing benefit; passkey copy is capability-neutral; prompts stay task- and person-centered; and camera permission copy uses the explicit Continue pre-alert and purpose strings.

Password fallback remains deliberate · P2-291 (accepted D-043 exception)

Was

Password sign-in and recovery remain sufficient without a second factor while passkeys are additive during rollout.

Now

No unapproved MFA or recovery change was invented. D-043 keeps password fallback available while passkeys are discoverable and phishing-resistant; requiring a second factor or removing password recovery needs a separate accepted security decision.

Scanner, kiosk, imagery, and full-screen surfaces · P1-235, P1-237, P2-271, P2-293, P2-295–297, P2-307, P2-325–327

Was

Black camera/photo surfaces could leave system chrome unreadable, scanner-burst filtering rejected normal Paste, drag/drop, and Scribble, the dedicated kiosk had no status-bar reveal, loading/retry and failed thumbnails were unclear, full-screen photos lost context and recovery, content surfaces used the wrong material, and zoom depended on touch gestures alone.

Now

Camera and photo viewers keep readable status-bar treatment and recovery controls; native kiosk text editing accepts standard UIKit input; loading states and retry paths are named; failed thumbnails keep a recoverable surface; bundled raster artwork retains its declared scale variants with sRGB metadata; full-screen photos retain context, VoiceOver-adjustable zoom, visible zoom controls, and Reduce Motion behavior; content-layer surfaces use the app surface system; and kiosk landscape remains an explicit platform deadline rather than a hidden claim.

Feedback, notifications, and discovery · P2-241–246, P2-263–264, P2-316–324

Was

Custom fills could lose contrast in Dark Mode, banners moved with Reduce Motion, skeletons and toasts were silent to VoiceOver, invalid symbols could render blank, profile uploads were device-scaled, Mark All Read and crop reset were irreversible, notification prompts and interruption behavior were generic, snooze state could diverge, categories repeated the tap destination, TipKit cadence and eligibility were implicit, and chart selection marks multiplied across a series.

Now

Semantic tokens adapt to contrast; motion respects Reduce Motion; loading and transient feedback announce through accessibility; all requested symbols resolve against the installed catalog; profile uploads render at the declared 1024-pixel target; mark-all and crop reset have undo; notification prompts follow demonstrated interest, foreground delivery avoids redundant interruption, snooze reconciles with account state, categories explain hidden previews without duplicate actions, TipKit cadence and eligibility are explicit, and chart selection creates one inspection mark per selected day.

State, refresh, and system shell · P2-298, P2-300–302, P2-311–312, P2-328

Was

Older refresh responses could overwrite newer state, unavailable context commands stayed visible but disabled, useful identifiers were not consistently selectable, the system launch frame carried branding, accent calls bypassed the established token, and the custom month calendar assumed left-to-right ordering.

Now

Latest-request generation guards protect Home, Notifications, Event Detail, Trade Board, and Overdue refreshes; unavailable commands hide; operational text is selectable; the system launch frame is neutral while sign-in retains brand identity; accent usage routes through Wisconsin-red and semantic tokens; and calendar weekday order, first weekday, and swipe direction follow the current locale.
