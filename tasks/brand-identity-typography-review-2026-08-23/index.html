What changed, and why

This is an identity correction, not a global font swap. The body remains Geist and the new treatment sets no size, weight, tracking, color, or layout property.

Primary row identity had no semantic font role

Was

Rows outside semantic headings and card titles inherited the body face, so booking names, selected equipment, report links, and some person-name rows quietly drifted to Geist.

Now

A family-only .brand-identity treatment gives those nouns the same Gotham role already used by page, detail, card, item-table, booking-list, and user-list identity.

Shared seams now carry the decision

Was

Parallel surfaces restated only weight and size, leaving font choice accidental and route-specific.

Now

The shared Item title and report entity link carry Gotham, with direct hooks for item booking history, equipment selection/review, organization rows, overdue rankings, and app-activity people rows.

Supporting information stays quiet

Was

A broad container-level font change would also brand dates, statuses, descriptions, and controls, making dense operational screens noisier.

Now

Only the identifying noun changes family. The source contract guards the body face and representative metadata lines.
