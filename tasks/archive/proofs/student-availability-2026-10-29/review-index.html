Native iOS

After-only: no matched pre-change native capture was retained. Existing offline Availability scenario, WisconsinPerformance, iPhone 18 Pro Max on iOS 27.0. Fixture dates are relative to the recorded October 29 capture. No live API writes or push delivery are exercised.

What changed, and why

A painted week saves together

Was

One request per weekday could leave a partially saved week after failure.

Now

One validated batch and one serializable transaction create all selected weekdays with their audits and conflict refresh.

Review applies to the current request

Was

A full stale form could replace dates during a decision, and student edits could retain an earlier approval.

Now

Staff decisions send only status, note and version. Student edits return time off to pending; a stale supplied version yields 409 and retains the draft.

Scheduling checks the full call window

Was

A same-day wall-clock comparison missed availability on overnight windows.

Now

The shared evaluator covers every local date, exact end boundaries, spring-forward gaps and repeated fall-back hours.
