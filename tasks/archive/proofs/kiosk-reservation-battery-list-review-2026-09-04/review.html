What changed, and why

Quantity was presented as identity

Was

Unscanned reservation slots were shown as #1, #2, and so on, even though the pickup scan service accepts any available unit from the reserved family.

Now

Numbered placeholders are grouped into one quantity row. The slot IDs still drive confirmation, while actual scanned unit tags remain visible for custody.

Battery guidance now answers the likely question

Was

The list did not explain whether the printed number had to match.

Now

The Kiosk explicitly says to scan any available units and that printed unit numbers do not need to match the list.
