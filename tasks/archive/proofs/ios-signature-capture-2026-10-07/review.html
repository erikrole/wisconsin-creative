iPad Signature Capture

Staff at a media day open a team roster on the iPad, find whoever walks up, and capture an Apple Pencil signature in the same layout they know from the web.

New screens (after only)

These screens did not exist in the iOS app before, so there is no baseline to pair. Captured by `SignatureScreenshotUITests` on a dedicated iPad Air 13-inch (M4) simulator, iOS 27.0, light appearance, fixture scenarios `signature-collections`, `signature-roster`, `signature-capture` (fictional names; synthetic ink from the web's pen-preview sample). Landscape frames were rotated upright from the native-portrait framebuffer.

What changed, and why

Capture moves from Safari to the app

Was

Pencil samples reached the page through Safari's coalesced Pointer Events; the 2026-27 captures recorded about half of them twice.

Now

UIKit coalesced touches deliver each Pencil sample once with its timestamp; predicted touches only hide latency and are never saved. Repeated runs are still dropped, with the web's rule.

History is one big bar

Was

Small icon buttons, and Clear could only be undone through a separate state.

Now

Large Undo, Redo, Clear, and Replay sit together above the canvas. Undo restores a Clear. Pencil double-tap or squeeze and Command-Z also undo.

Replay before saving

Was

No way to see the signature drawn back.

Now

Replay redraws the unsaved strokes at the signer's real Pencil pace, with pen lifts capped at half a second.

Things to know

  • Fixture captures prove layout only. The acceptance gate is a physical iPad + Pencil save; see `docs/IOS_DEVICE_WALKTHROUGH.md`.
  • Unsaved ink lives in memory only; leaving capture or the app being killed loses it (the web keeps device-local drafts).
  • Replaying a saved signature needs pen timing stored on the server (plan slice 2).
