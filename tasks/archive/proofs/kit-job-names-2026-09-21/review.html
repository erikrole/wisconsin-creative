Football kit job names

Football video jobs now use the same all-caps names as the kits: SLOW1, SLOW2, BENCH, and ROAM1 through ROAM4.

What changed, and why

Job picker

Was

Slow 1, Slow 2, Bench, Roam 1, Roam 2, Roam 3, Roam 4.

Now

SLOW1, SLOW2, BENCH, ROAM1, ROAM2, ROAM3, ROAM4. Choosing a job still fills that name.

Calling a kit

Was

A SLOW1 kit with a job attached was labeled Slow 1.

Now

The same kit is labeled SLOW1. A kit whose name differs from the job still shows the job first, then the kit name.

Things to know

  • PHOTO1 and SLOW2 still both include FB 70-200 1. Photo kits stay un-roled.
