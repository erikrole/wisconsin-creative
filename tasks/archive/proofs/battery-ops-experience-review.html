What changed, and why

The live number was easy to misread as an editable field

Was

A dense wall of numbered tiles carried the truth but offered no concise explanation of how the count changes.

Now

The family says that numbered availability comes from unit status, then routes new physical stock through Add units and count corrections through each unit menu.

Receiving lost product identity

Was

Battery Ops could add the next numbered range, but could not attach an existing product to those units during receiving.

Now

The receiving dialog keeps the exact range, before/after count, and reason while adding an optional active-product assignment.

Metadata was hidden behind an unlabeled family link

Was

Location and category were present, while threshold, QR identity, label readiness, product mix, and the edit destination were not grouped as an operational summary.

Now

The family workspace exposes those fields together and provides an explicit Edit metadata handoff to the existing audited detail flow.
