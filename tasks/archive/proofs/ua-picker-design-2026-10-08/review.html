What changed, and why

Standard issue stays separate

Was

A row of nine thumbnails took priority over shopping.

Now

The kit remains one click away, with a clear explanation that it does not use the allowance.

Names, prices and selection each have a place

Was

Small cards placed names beside prices and tiny swatches over photos. A border was the main selection signal.

Now

Wider cards stack name and price, use 44-pixel swatch targets, and label selected products with a check and picked quantity.

Review becomes a place to finish

Was

Staff could inspect or remove picks, but had to return to cards for size changes.

Now

Size and quantity controls update the draft and budget immediately. Controls lock during saves, and invalid sizes still block submission.

Verification

  • 9 isolated Chromium browser checks pass, including save/reload regressions, keyboard kit expansion, mobile filters, and inline review edits.
  • The new inline-review regression fails on the preserved pre-design source because the controls do not exist.
  • Application build, changed-file lint, docs verification, local links and diff checks passed. Standalone TypeScript reports the same two existing iOS test errors; authenticated preview remains pending.

Things to know

  • No commit, push or deployment. Changes remain on codex/ua-picker-hardening.
  • The profile pick list still receives no edit callbacks and remains read-only. The shared color swatches are larger in the product preview too.
  • Synthetic API responses prove client interactions only. They do not prove authenticated sessions or persisted database writes.
  • The existing standalone TypeScript errors are in two unrelated iOS test files; no managed preview exists for this branch.
  • Wording corrected to Standard issue, covered by the department separately from the pick allowance. Historical before captures retain their original wording.
