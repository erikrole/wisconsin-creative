Native reservation Gear on system chrome

Gear search, category, cart, and conflicts now use searchable, toolbar, swipe, and badge APIs instead of a handmade field, chip bar, and cart inset.

After-only fixture evidence

iPhone 16 Pro (27.0) UDID E055DEBC-12B1-4E0F-BE11-1CFFF4A454DE, WisconsinPerformance create-booking-events, light appearance. Bottom toolbar is empty-state hidden until an item is selected. The 6:30 AM device screenshot is live inventory in dark mode, so it is not a matched before pair.

What changed, and why

Search

Was

A handmade TextField in a top inset, with .toolbar(removing: .search) to suppress iOS 27’s bottom search accessory.

Now

.searchable in the navigation bar drawer, with .searchFocused so search-select-search can keep the keyboard after an add.

Category and cart

Was

All/Cameras/Lenses/Batteries/Other lived in the search field. Selected gear was a custom bottom bar.

Now

Category is a trailing toolbar menu beside Scan. Selected count and Review are ToolbarItem(placement: .bottomBar) and appear after the first add.

Rows

Was

Custom plus/Unavailable chrome, no swipe, no item peek.

Now

Swipe Add/Remove, long-press View item, and a red alert in the add slot for conflicts. The plus stays gray on every addable row.
