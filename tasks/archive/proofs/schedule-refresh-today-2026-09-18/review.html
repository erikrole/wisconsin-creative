What changed, and why

Reload position

Was

Refresh restored raw window.scrollY, including 0 from the error boundary and loading skeleton, so the list opened at the archive floor.

Now

Refresh restores the visible event only when it is still in this window. Otherwise it opens on today.

Older-event loading

Was

The past sentinel sat in view on first paint and walked every older page until May, while a sticky day header overlapped the first row.

Now

Older pages load after the reader scrolls toward them. My shifts still fills remaining pages in the background without yanking the view to the top.
