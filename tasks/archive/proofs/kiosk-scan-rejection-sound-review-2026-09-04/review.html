What changed, and why

A blocking scan no longer looks accepted

Was

The scan endpoint could succeed and the client staged the item before the future reservation conflict was known, leaving a misleading cart count and a blocked completion step.

Now

Checkout serializes candidates and preflights the candidate cart before insertion; a blocking reservation, checkout, unavailable-asset, or bulk-shortage result is rejected and never increments the visible count.

Failure feedback works in the assembly line

Was

The operator had to notice a visual error banner or haptic while continuing through a rapid scan sequence.

Now

Rejected, failed, and duplicate feedback retains the visual, haptic, and VoiceOver announcement and adds a short procedural failure cue; successful scans and non-blocking warnings stay quiet.
