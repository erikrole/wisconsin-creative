Schedule toolbar and filters

Staff and students on /schedule get a compact command row, searchable sport, a real Filters panel, and Clear that leaves My shifts alone.

Visual comparison

After-only. No trustworthy before capture: the checkout already had Schedule infinite-scroll work, and authenticated /schedule was login-gated (307 to /login) during this pass. Do not treat this page as matched visual proof.

What changed, and why

Text comparison only. Captures were not available.

Command row wrapping

Was

View, a five-way Venue toggle, Sport, a My Shifts switch, and Filters all sat on one wrapping sticky row.

Now

View, a My shifts pressed button, searchable Sport, Filters, and Clear. Venue moved into the panel.

Filters panel

Was

Nested FilterChips inside a popover for Area and Coverage, with Venue always on the bar.

Now

Venue, Area, and Coverage are 40px toggle groups. Older records stays a labeled switch with explanation.

Clear and My shifts

Was

Clear all lived only inside the popover and treated student-default My shifts as a filter, so Clear turned their default off.

Now

Clear is on the bar and resets sport, venue, area, coverage, older records, queue, and date windows. My shifts stays a preference.

Recovery

Was

Date-window deep links had no chip. Week and calendar empty states mentioned filters with no action.

Now

Date windows get a removable chip. List, week, and calendar empty states can clear the same filters.
