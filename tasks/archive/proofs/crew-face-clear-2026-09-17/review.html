What changed, and why

The X covered the face

Was

A 16px (earlier 28px) disc sat on the photo with only a 4px offset, so most of the badge covered the avatar or the plus.

Now

An 18px disc is centered on the trailing-top corner of a shared 24px face frame. Empty slots overlay that frame on Assign’s plus.

The X was always in the way

Was

Visible at rest, and a hidden-but-present control could still steal the Assign click.

Now

Opacity 0 and pointer-events none at rest on pointer screens. Row hover, row focus-within, and keyboard focus reveal it. Overflow menus keep Unassign and Remove.

Verification

  • Playwright hover on the assigned Erik Role row: opacity 0 at rest, 1 on hover. Crop 1070×45.
  • Playwright hover on an open Student slot: overlay box matches the plus (24×24 at the same origin); clear center is the plus top-right. Opacity 0 at rest, 1 on hover.
  • Authenticated Event detail rest screenshot: pluses and avatars are clean; no X visible.
  • Did not click Unassign or Remove on the live Football vs Eastern Michigan crew.
  • tests/schedule-ui-polish-source.test.ts: 7 passed. ESLint clean on crew-row.tsx and WorkingCrewEditor.tsx.
  • `npx tsc --noEmit` still fails only on unrelated src/lib/services/blasts.ts payload Json.

Things to know

  • These pairs compare rest vs hover of the new badge. The previous covering-X state was not snapshotted in an isolated baseline.
  • Badge close-ups badge-assigned-close.png and badge-placeholder-close.png are 24×25 hit-target crops, not matched pairs.
  • Native iOS Event detail is unchanged.
