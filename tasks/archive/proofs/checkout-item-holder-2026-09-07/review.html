What changed, and why

Real handoffs were invisible

Was

A whole checkout could transfer owners, but a camera or lens swapped between people still appeared under the original manifest with no correction path.

Now

Staff/Admin can use Assign holder or Change holder on one active serialized row and optionally record handoff context.

Correction could not weaken checkout truth

Was

Moving equipment between checkouts would risk fragmenting scan, allocation, event, and return history.

Now

The mutation changes only assignedUserId/assignedAt, advances the booking snapshot, and writes an audit entry in one serializable transaction.

Shared remains the safe default

Was

The travel-case workflow deliberately avoided falsely assigning pooled gear to a person.

Now

A blank holder still inherits personal-owner or shared-manifest custody; only serialized exceptions are attributed, while bulk gear stays pooled.

Things to know

  • No Notre Dame production checkout was changed, and this source has not been committed, pushed, or deployed.
  • My Gear, accountability, reports, native iOS, and kiosk presentation do not yet treat explicit item holders as their primary attribution; that is the next bounded slice under GAP-78.
