What changed, and why

Field chrome is one contract

Was

Input, Textarea, NativeSelect, and SelectTrigger used near-copies of focus/invalid styles with drifting transitions and disabled treatment.

Now

Shared fieldChrome covers hover, focus, invalid, and disabled states so a text field and a select in the same row match.

Press, focus, and reduced motion

Was

Buttons already scaled on press. Duration, cursor, and reduced-motion were inconsistent across toggles, menus, and overlays.

Now

Buttons, toggles, and overlay close controls use a 150ms press scale, 40px close targets, and motion-reduce safety. Menus and selects open from their trigger origin.

Compact controls keep a 40px hit area

Was

Checkbox, radio, and switch visuals were 16–20px with no expanded hit target.

Now

They keep their compact visuals and expand to a 40px hit area. Switch is a slightly larger 24×44 track. Form fields stay h-9 until a dedicated field-baseline slice.

Sentence-case table headers

Was

TableHead forced uppercase tracking-wider, so some headers shouted (STATUS) next to sentence-case sort labels.

Now

Headers are sentence case and medium weight, matching the design-language table rule.

Overlays and tooltips

Was

Dialog titles could sit under the close control. Sheets opened over 500ms. Tooltips always waited 200ms.

Now

Dialogs cap height and pad titles away from the 40px close control. Sheets open in 300ms. After the first tooltip, the next one skips delay.
