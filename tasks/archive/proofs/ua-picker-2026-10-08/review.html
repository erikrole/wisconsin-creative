What changed, and why

Keep the save version tied to the edited list

Was

A background refresh could update the version while leaving older local edits in place.

Now

Unsaved edits retain their base version and newer server versions block saving until an explicit reload.

Preserve choices through failures

Was

Failed reads could unmount the form or clear dirty state; controls remained editable during saves.

Now

Failed reads retain choices, in-flight edits are blocked, and successful saves adopt the acknowledged list.
