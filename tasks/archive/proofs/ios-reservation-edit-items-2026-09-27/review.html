Edit Reservation Items

People who own a reservation can now add or remove items from the Edit Booking sheet instead of going to the web app.

After (no matched before)

After-only. I did not build a matching before capture. Before, this sheet had no Items row, and its footnote said gear was read-only on the phone. Captured on iPhone 18 Pro Max, iOS 27, light mode, fixture scenarios booking-edit-reservation and booking-edit-items.

What changed, and why

Items on the Edit Booking sheet

Was

Gear was read-only on iOS. Changing it meant going to the web app.

Now

Reservations show an Items row with a count. Tapping it opens the same picker as New Reservation, with a Done button.

Unavailable items are blocked

Was

—

Now

The picker checks availability without counting this reservation against itself, so conflicting items can't be added. Save stays disabled while items conflict, are still being checked, or none are left.

Checkouts unchanged

Was

Kiosk only.

Now

Still kiosk only. The Items row appears only on reservations.

Partly picked-up reservations

Was

Picked-up items could be removed, and supply counts could be capped below the units already checked out. The server then rejected the save.

Now

Picked-up items are locked in the picker. Supply limits count this reservation's held units again, and the Items row shows 'N picked up'.

Return-time check

Was

Checked the saved items even after they were edited.

Now

Checks the edited items.
