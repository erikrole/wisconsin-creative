Native reservation Gear rows, filters, and conflicts

Gear browse now leads with the asset tag, puts category on the search bar, and paints known conflicts as blocked instead of a faded plus.

After-only fixture evidence

iPhone 16 Pro (27.0) UDID E055DEBC-12B1-4E0F-BE11-1CFFF4A454DE, WisconsinPerformance scenario create-booking-events, light appearance. Erik’s 6:30 AM screenshot is physical-device dark mode with live inventory, so it is not a matched before pair.

What changed, and why

Identity subline

Was

Every browse row repeated the product name and Camp Randall under the tag, so the list read as a catalog instead of grab-and-go gear.

Now

Browse rows keep the asset tag. Product and location stay in VoiceOver. Needed-next and conflict copy remain the only supporting lines.

Category chips

Was

All, Cameras, Lenses, Batteries, and Other sat as a second control strip under search.

Now

Those categories are a menu on the search field, with a checkmark on the current choice.

Conflict chrome

Was

A conflicted lens kept a plus-shaped mark and a faded red caption, so it still looked tappable.

Now

Conflicted rows use a red rail, red fill, red title, the booking plus window, and an Unavailable badge. They cannot be newly selected.
