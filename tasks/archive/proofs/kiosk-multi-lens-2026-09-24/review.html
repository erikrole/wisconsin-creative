What changed

Fixes by severity

Custody

Records stay true

  • Only the owner, staff, or a shared-case operator can edit or remove items on a live checkout. Anyone else returns by scanning.
  • A failed checkout can no longer resend an old cart and wipe new scans. Scanning waits until the saved handoff settles, and Discard clears it.
  • Abandoned carts expire after 20 minutes instead of reopening the next day.
  • Counted stock (cables, tape) can finally be returned at the kiosk.
  • Leftover battery pickups no longer fail, and partial pickups are no longer cancelled as no-shows.
  • Shared travel-case holders stay private in scan messages.
Operator-blocking

The counter doesn't get stuck

  • An expired or unreadable saved handoff is rejected cleanly instead of blocking that person on that iPad forever.
  • A dropped availability check has a Retry button.
  • Removing an item mid-scan no longer rejects the scan.
  • Staged batteries can be replaced before pickup.
  • Inactivity no longer strands the iPad on a signed-out screen during activation.
  • Fast scan bursts on idle, hub and pickup are queued, not dropped, and every rejection is audible.
Design

Opinionated cleanup

  • Checkout details fits one screen. The person moved into the header and the gameday kit, which was below the fold, is one menu row.
  • The scanner pill shows only where it's the only signal, so it no longer sits on each flow's right rail.
  • The idle device-status button lives in the header instead of covering it.
  • Battery rules are stated once, with correct plurals. Hub overdue is a count.
  • Activation's red belongs to the confirm key. Empty event rows are hidden.
