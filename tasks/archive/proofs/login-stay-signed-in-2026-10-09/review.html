Stay Signed In

Account creation (after only)

Not shown as a pair because the new checkbox makes the two images different heights. Baseline image: before-onboarding.png, with receipt before-onboarding-evidence.json.

What changed, and why

Sessions ended on a fixed clock

Was

Expiry was set at sign-in and never moved. That meant 12 hours by default, 30 days when remembered, and 30 days for everyone on iPhone.

Now

Each use pushes expiry back out (D-066). Remembered sessions get 30 idle days with a 90-day cap. Unremembered ones get 12 idle hours, a 7-day cap, and end when the browser closes.

Remember me was opt-in and missing on account creation

Was

The box started unticked and was hidden while creating an account, so every new account began with a 12-hour session.

Now

"Keep me signed in" starts ticked and shows on every step. Account creation honors it, and native sign-up defaults to remembered.

Signing back in lost your place

Was

A signed-out deep link went to /login and then to the home page.

Now

The page you asked for is carried as a validated same-origin returnTo. In the after run, a mocked password sign-in from /login?returnTo=/items?q=lens navigated to /items?q=lens. The before run went to /.

Password managers can offer passkeys

Was

There was no passkey endpoints document.

Now

/.well-known/passkey-endpoints sends 1Password, Chrome and Apple Passwords to Settings → Security, where passkeys are created and managed.

Verification

  • Before run: box unticked, no box during account creation, login sent rememberMe false, landed on /.
  • After run: box ticked, box present during account creation, login sent rememberMe true, landed on /items?q=lens.
  • vitest: all 750 files pass (5,605 tests), including the new sliding-session and returnTo tests.
  • npx tsc --noEmit, eslint on the changed files, npm run build:app, npm run verify:docs, and db:migrate:check all pass.

Things to know

  • Captures use mocked API responses. They show the screens and what the client sends, not real sessions.
  • Migration 0171 is not deployed. No authenticated preview proof yet; that needs the PR's managed preview (GAP-88).
  • Passkey device proof (GAP-62) is still open.
