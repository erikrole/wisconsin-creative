What changed, and why

The team view did not exist

Was

A person’s record lived inside a profile, so there was no place to compare coverage or see an aggregate season.

Now

The new /scoreboard page leads with unique events, the team record, work credits, contributors, sport totals, and a sortable leaderboard.

Totals could be easy to misread

Was

Adding individual event or game counts together would overstate how many events the team covered.

Now

Unique team events and games stay separate from person-event and person-game credits, with the counting method stated on both clients.

A tiny perfect sample could look like the leader

Was

A one-game 100% record could outrank sustained work if rate alone drove the board.

Now

Win-rate ranking requires three resolved games; Events and Wins remain available as explicit alternatives.

Universal Scoreboard access could have widened People access

Was

Routing through User Detail would have coupled shared metrics to private profile, contact, schedule, booking, and activity data.

Now

Leaderboard links go directly to a dedicated shared Scoreboard route carrying only id, name, avatar, and metrics. Existing collaborator capability gates remain intact for every other destination.
