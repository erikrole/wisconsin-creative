What changed, and why

Partial batches retain unfinished work

Was

The handoff unmounted the form; Fix remaining reopened blank.

Now

Intake stays mounted and hidden during the handoff. Retained keys and error focus stay valid; hidden intake cannot resubmit. Leaving unfinished rows requires confirmation.

Copy resets are deliberate

Was

Start a different item or retry source could clear edited details immediately.

Now

Keep editing preserves the copied draft and returns focus; confirmation clears it.

Quantity switches preserve new intake

Was

Add to existing cleared the staged image while other create fields survived.

Now

New-item details and image survive the round trip; adjusting existing stock keeps its record image unchanged.

Copy notices stay readable

Was

Descriptions after a title occupied the 1rem icon column.

Now

Scoped creation notices use the full content column.
