What changed, and why

Reliable text entry

Was

Draft tracking ran during input capture and could reset text before its change handler read it. Reproduced in the authenticated preview.

Now

Both forms record draft changes after field handlers have accepted input.

Protected tracking changes

Was

Changing tracking style could discard entered details immediately.

Now

An edited draft requires Clear draft and switch. Keep editing preserves values; unchanged choices remain quick.

Clearer choices and feedback

Was

Technical tracking badges and forced focus into the form.

Now

Visible examples, responsive cards, keyboard navigation within the choices, and announced footer progress.
