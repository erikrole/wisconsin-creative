What changed, and why

Applicant identity

Was

Small name with truncation and every specialty competing as a badge.

Now

Wrapping semibold name, one primary specialty badge, and secondary areas as quieter text.

Evidence at a glance

Was

Small summary text and unlabeled material icons.

Now

Three-line readable summary, labeled material indicators, direct source links, and rating count.

Controls and board layout

Was

Review control competed with plain source links in a compact card.

Now

Separate review footer with saving state, more column spacing, explicit empty columns, and local horizontal scrolling.

Verification

  • 183 focused Hiring/Workforce tests and 11 CLI tests passed.
  • Keyboard opening and review toggle passed in the actual card fixture; no browser errors.
  • 390 CSS-pixel mobile fixture: page width remained 390 pixels; card contents fit their containers.
  • Dark appearance and missing-data/long-name fixtures visually inspected.
  • Production data independently verified: 76 candidates, 55 Applied, 5 Round 1, 16 Passed; 1 Maybe note. Blank sheet decisions and review flags preserved.

Things to know

  • The card redesign is local and has not been committed, pushed or deployed.
  • The merged Workforce branch has no managed preview environment. Authenticated server proof of the changed UI remains pending.
  • Source snapshots and matching metadata are retained alongside this review. Mobile and dark captures are supplemental after-only evidence.
