What changed, and why

A blocked scan now has an explicit recovery path

Was

After understanding who held the item and when it was due back, the operator still had to decide how to recover from the blocked add.

Now

A timing-resolvable conflict row presents three direct choices: remove the staged item, edit the return time, or re-arm scanning for another item/unit; other blocking states show the applicable safe subset.

Changing the return time keeps availability trustworthy

Was

A populated cart could return from checkout details without a clearly documented fresh availability check for the new due time.

Now

The cart is preserved while details reopen, and returning to scanning refreshes availability against the edited return time before completion can proceed.
