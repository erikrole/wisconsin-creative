What changed, and why

A sport filter could not answer venue or matchup questions

Was

The aggregate view could narrow only by sport, so records at a venue, against a team, or by Home/Away/Neutral site required a different surface or mental arithmetic.

Now

Sport, Schedule venue, opponent, and site are first-class exact dimensions. One value per dimension stacks with AND semantics on both web and native.

Independent numbers could drift into different scopes

Was

Adding dimensions client-side would risk filtering the team record without filtering work totals or person rankings the same way.

Now

The server filters the two bounded event sets once, then derives totals, four breakdowns, and every person summary from that shared intersection.

Narrowing could collapse the next menu

Was

If filter choices came from the current result, selecting one dimension could make valid choices in another disappear and strand the user.

Now

Facets always come from the complete bounded window. Empty intersections are recoverable, and removing one filter leaves every other selection intact.

The numbers had no narrative layer

Was

A reader could inspect totals, but the interface did not name the particular combination or surface its leading contributor as a reusable fact.

Now

Snapshot turns every current intersection into a compact, deterministic story—the first presentation layer for future recap cards, superlatives, and streaks.

Things to know

  • Production was not changed. The installed signed-in native build reaches the canonical host, which still returns 404 for the undeployed aggregate route; live native totals and filter interaction remain a post-deploy GAP-71 gate.
  • Venue is the cleaned Schedule venue derived from raw calendar location text, never an equipment pickup location. Site is the canonical Home/Away/Neutral event classification.
  • The future end-of-year experience is product direction, not shipped scope here. Any superlative, streak, or surprising combination must remain reproducible from the same event authority, counting rules, and privacy allowlist.
  • No schema, migration, commit, push, deployment, or public Scoreboard publishing was performed.
