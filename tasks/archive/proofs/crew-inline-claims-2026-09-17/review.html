Inline student claim review on crew rows

Staff and Admins should see who has claimed an open Student slot or a posted trade on the crew row itself, then review Approve or Decline without leaving Event detail.

After-only

No trustworthy matched pair. The checkout already carried Event authoring and crew dirty work, and no isolated baseline was captured before this slice. After captures: after-crew-row.png (Ben Xiong’s claim on the first Video Student slot) and after-review-popover.png (Admin Approve/Decline popover). Role: ADMIN on Preview Football vs Eastern Michigan. Live claim was opened, not approved or declined.

What changed, and why

Pending claims lived only on Trade Board queues

Was

Event detail crew showed an empty Assign control. Admins had to leave for Trade Board to see who claimed the slot.

Now

The working-copy crew row shows an avatar group of claimants and a Review popover with Approve/Decline. Multiple students on one slot stack as a group; approving one declines the rest.

Claimed trades were a badge, not a face

Was

Schedule students saw Trade claimed without a claimant avatar. Staff crew rows had no inline trade request.

Now

A claimed trade attaches the claimant to the assigned row with the same Review popover.
