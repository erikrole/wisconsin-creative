What changed, and why

“Unavailable” hid the person and deadline

Was

A blocked serialized item surfaced only generic unavailable/conflict copy, so the operator could not tell who had the camera or when it would return.

Now

The server includes optional requester, booking-kind, and status metadata; the Kiosk turns that into a direct person/item/deadline message and preserves a safe fallback for older responses.

Reservation and active custody now read differently

Was

A reservation and an open checkout looked the same in the scan cart.

Now

Reserved, Checked Out, and Pending Pickup use state-specific labels, semantic status colors, and matching VoiceOver/accessibility text.
