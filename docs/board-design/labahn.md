## Main

- The main is tiny. Size type for it at 100% on the native comp; a design scaled down from a large main will not read.
- The board size was once taken from last season's graphic and was wrong. Take it from the manifest every time.
- PlayerID and similar families render two versions: the native `PlayerID - LaBahn - Main` and an HD `PlayerID - LaBahn - Main 1920x1080`. Build the HD version as its own comp, not as a scaled nest of the native one, and not on the North Board main (its safe-zone offset is wrong for this).
- The main intro slot takes footage at native pixels, so stills for it must be wide HD frames with the subject in the middle band.
- Clips from Premiere arrive already letterboxed; don't grade over them again.
- Some sponsor stills have been delivered as HD Photoshop files by choice even though the board is far smaller. That is the requester's call; when it happens, design for the small screen anyway (big title, four short lines at most).
