# Signatures Area Scope (V1 Local Implementation)

## Document Control

- Area: Signatures
- Owner: Wisconsin Athletics Creative Product
- Last Updated: 2026-08-25

- Version: V1.0

## Direction

Make collecting team signatures a fast, reliable iPad workflow: roster identity first, pen-only capture second, server-confirmed artifact state third.

## Core Rules

1. A collection is uniquely identified by canonical collection code and season. Men’s Basketball uses `MBB`, Football uses `FB`, Volleyball uses `VB`, Men’s Hockey uses `MHKY`, Women’s Hockey uses `WHKY`, Women’s Basketball uses `WBB`, and Wrestling uses `WRES`; Creative staff use `CREATIVE`; Administration uses the standalone `ADMIN` collection; one-off manual signers use `ADHOC` with their sport/category stored on the member.
2. External roster members are separate from internal Gear Tracker users. Creative staff are linked internal full-time users whose primary/assigned area is Video, Photo, or Graphics, or whose job title explicitly identifies Creative or Digital Media; they are represented by separate signature-member records in the standalone Creative staff collection. A uniquely matched same-season team-staff record shares that internal identity and resolves the same canonical Creative Staff capture, so saving or removing it from either roster updates both surfaces without copying private artifacts.
3. Only staff/admin users can access the area. Students and collaborators are denied by the centralized permission map.
4. Team completeness is derived from active student-athletes with a committed current artifact revision. Student-athletes always require a signature and drive the primary progress bar; coaching and support staff are optional secondary work shown in a quieter signed/total count and never change team completeness. Standalone Creative staff and Administration members are included by default and can be excluded individually by an admin.
5. Roster imports are snapshots, not destructive syncs. MBB, Football, Volleyball, Men’s Hockey, Women’s Hockey, Women’s Basketball, and Wrestling each use an allowlisted UWBadgers source adapter; Administration uses the fixed `https://uwbadgers.com/staff-directory/administration-department/1` source and imports directory profiles as required support-staff members in `ADMIN`. Football and Volleyball map the `2026-27` season to the source site's `2026` roster URL segment, while the other four use the full `2026-27` segment. Wrestling weight classes remain player metadata and its jersey number is nullable. Apply is explicit, versioned, and never deletes historical members or captures. When a current `PLAYER` source ID changes, apply may transfer a safe historical capture to the new member on a unique exact normalized-name match; jersey numbers are advisory and do not participate in that identity key. The target must be blank, the historical member must be inactive, and ambiguous names, active saves, conflicting captures, or erased history fail closed for review. Player `Hometown` values from the official roster seed blank athlete-profile fields on apply, while an existing manual value is preserved. Creative Staff reconciliation starts automatically when `/signatures` mounts through the existing mutation endpoint; collection-list GET stays read-only so route prefetch cannot change roster state. Reconciliation remains version-checked and audits only real changes. It links team staff only on a unique exact normalized-name match among active eligible internal users and fails closed for players, ad-hoc members, ambiguity, or an existing conflicting link; standalone Administration members are never linked into Creative Staff or team collections.
6. Capture input is normalized strokes. The server owns SVG/PNG generation, crop bounds, hashes, clean signer filenames, and private storage paths. PNG exports are transparent RGBA, preserve aspect ratio, render up to 1600 x 900, and are never less than 1000px wide. SVG exports contain sanitized vector paths rather than embedded raster data. New stroke captures conservatively omit tiny isolated marks outside the substantive signature while preserving one-point-only signatures and nearby short marks. The signature line is deliberately uniform: there is no pressure, tilt, velocity, or end tapering. Because the tight crop is scaled into the configured export box, the rendered stroke width is normalized against that same export scale, so one configured thickness delivers one line weight whether a signer signs small or large. The multiplier is clamped so a degenerate crop degrades toward the configured width rather than a hairline or a blob, and committed revisions keep the width already baked into their stored SVG.
7. A pending or failed save is never presented as complete or downloadable.
8. Durable capture and artifact state remains in signature tables; the general audit log is supplemental evidence and is subject to its 90-day retention policy.
9. The active collection chooser hides archived seasons by default. Admins may reveal, archive, and restore historical collections; archived collections remain read-only. Each collection card exposes Download All, Archive/Restore, and Delete from its compact overflow menu. Download All offers separate private ZIPs of current committed PNG or SVG revisions, selected by format, with clean collision-safe signer filenames. Collection deletion is admin-only, version-checked, confirms only when the roster has captured signatures, invalidates the collection before private-artifact cleanup, and keeps an archived retry state if cleanup fails.
10. Roster presentation keeps players in jersey-number order, renders actual player numerals in the licensed Wisconsin Athletics `WIsconsin-Regular` face, keeps imported coaching/support staff and Administration members in the order preserved by the UWBadgers source snapshot, and sorts the standalone Creative staff roster by last name. Staff, Administration, and Creative rows retain the person icon instead of a synthetic number.
11. Signature rosters use fixed-height horizontal rows with Person, Signature, and compact Actions rails. Person names use Gotham Black. A committed capture renders as a compact private PNG button that opens an authenticated Quick Look titled with the signer name and equal-height PNG/SVG downloads; an incomplete member renders the centered capture action in the same slot. Creative Staff rows show the person name only; imported players show normalized position and academic year (for example, `Guard • Senior`) beneath the name, while other team members retain their one-line source title. Player groups are labeled Student-Athletes, team staff group labels use title case, and multi-digit jersey numerals use restrained positive tracking. Roster groups are independently collapsible and carry their own signed count, so the detail-level readiness card and per-row Requirement/Status labels are omitted. Required capture actions use the official `#c80000` RGB-red brand treatment, while excluded non-player capture actions remain equally sized and available with neutral styling. Signature previews use display-only theme filtering so transparent source PNG bytes render black in light mode and white in dark mode. Secondary file, non-player readiness, and lifecycle actions remain available from the 44px compact row action menu. Explicit PNG and SVG actions download attachments with clean `<signer>-signature` filenames; all previews and downloads remain authenticated.
12. Admin capture settings are collapsed by default and explain how ink color, line thickness, trim margin, and maximum dimensions affect generated PNG/SVG output. A live pen preview renders a representative signature through the same builder that produces the delivered artifact, reports the resulting transparent export size on a transparency ground, and warns when a setting combination a typical signature could not export. Saved signatures lock the fields but keep the preview visible, while collection reset remains available in a separated danger area behind a descriptive confirmation.
13. Successful recaptures keep prior immutable private revisions available from the row menu with clean versioned filenames. Remove signature, collection reset, and collection delete erase every retained revision in their scope; collection delete also removes the roster records after private cleanup succeeds.
14. Staff/admin can add an ad-hoc signer from `/signatures` by entering a name, sport/category, and season; creation is audited and opens the capture surface immediately on an iPad. Unsupported clients see a disabled Add-on-iPad action and cannot create an incomplete ad-hoc record through this surface. Replace actions follow the same gate.
15. Signature capture is iPad-only. Unsupported web clients show a disabled `Capture on iPad` action with a tooltip explaining that an iPad and Apple Pencil are required; direct desktop visits to the capture surface are blocked.
16. Multiple iPads may intake signatures concurrently. Drafts and their save request IDs are device-local, different members can save in parallel, and each member uses first-writer-wins capture versions so a stale second device receives an explicit conflict without losing its local draft. Reopening that member can recover a prior-version draft for deliberate review, but never reuses its old request ID; clear and successful save remove all same-member draft records. Replaying the same request is idempotent, including temporary `425`, `429`, and server-error responses; ambiguous uploads do not delete a committed artifact, abandoned upload/finalize operations are reclaimed by bounded cleanup, and a late upload that loses a delete/reset race self-fences and removes its own private paths.
17. Student-athlete members have a separate website-profile step after a committed signature. Full birthday and hometown are required; Instagram, TikTok, and X/Twitter handles are optional, stored as handles without links, and normalized without a leading `@`. Profile completeness does not change signature-artifact readiness. The roster marks incomplete player profiles, the member contract returns the normalized profile for website consumption, and existing signed players can edit it without recapturing. The hometown input also opts into browser city autofill and explains when its value came from the official roster. Coaching, support, Creative Staff, and ad-hoc members do not receive profile fields or this step.

## Runtime Surfaces

- `/signatures`: separate MBB, Football, Volleyball, Men’s Hockey, Women’s Hockey, Women’s Basketball, Wrestling, Creative staff, Administration, and ad-hoc collection cards, completeness indicators, compact collection actions (format-selectable PNG/SVG ZIP export, archive/restore, and delete), a sport- and directory-aware UWBadgers import entry point, and one-off signer creation.
- `/signatures/[collectionId]`: roster grid, settings, reconciliation, and file library.
- `/signatures/[collectionId]/capture/[memberId]`: full-height pen-only capture with signer identity at the top.
- `/api/signatures/collections/[collectionId]/members/[memberId]`: staff/admin-only, private no-store one-member capture bootstrap; it does not expose the rest of the roster or revision history.
- `/api/signatures/collections/[collectionId]/members/[memberId]/profile`: staff/admin, version-checked student-athlete website-profile mutation.
- `/api/signatures/*`: authenticated collection, import, capture, artifact, and cleanup contracts.
